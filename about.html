Oaza Cafe (أوزا كافيه) is a calm, modern coffee spot in Riyadh's Al-Sahafa district. Open 24 hours a day, it's a favorite for quality specialty coffee, signature drinks, and a relaxed atmosphere.

Guests praise the warm hospitality, the clean space, and the attentive staff — rated 4.5★ by 634+ reviewers on Google.
